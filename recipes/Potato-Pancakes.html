Potato Pancakes

Description

Potato pancakes, also known as latkes, are a delicious and crispy dish made from grated potatoes, onions, and eggs. They are typically fried until golden brown and served with sour cream or applesauce. These pancakes are a popular comfort food in many cultures and are especially enjoyed during Jewish holidays.

Ingredients

  • 2 Large eggs
  • 2 Tablespoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 6 medium potatoes, peeled and shredded
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil

Steps to cook

  1. gather all ingredients
  2. Beat eggs, flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper together in a large bowl; stir in potatoes and onion
  3. Heat oil in large skillet over medium heat. Drop heaping tablespoonfuls of potato mixture into hot oil in batches. Press to flatten.
  4. Cook until browned and crisp, about 3 minutes on each side.
  5. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
  6. Repeat with remaining potato mixture. Enjoy!
